Stop PDP buy-box bounce: remove competing bean-bag hero-stack layout

Headless measurement (Playwright + layout-shift API) proved the bean-bag PDP
was bouncing because two functions fought over #mc-pdp-features 283x in 9s:
mountPdpFeaturesBlock() kept it in the options column while buildBeanBagStack()
kept pulling it into #mc-pdp-hero-stack. Every move re-triggered the
MutationObserver, so it never settled (CLS-style oscillation, features/qty
moving ~12x each).

Fix: bean bags now use the SAME general two-column layout as every other
product (built once per runPatch). Removed buildBeanBagStack() entirely and the
stale "ordering owned by buildBeanBagStack" branch note. patchBeanBagPdp() is
reduced to the only bean-bag-specific, non-conflicting work: hide the duplicate
legacy price and park the cover swatches inside the option block (idempotent,
above Features, no competition for the slot before #mc-pdp-features).

Re-measured with the local build served over the live page: features/qty/swatch
now move once and settle (was 12x). Remaining CLS is page-level Volusion reflow
(manufacturer-logo area, related-content table) unrelated to the buy box.

Bumped cache version to pdp26.
