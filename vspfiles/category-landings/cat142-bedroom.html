BUYING GUIDE

HOW TO PLAN A BEDROOM FURNITURE LAYOUT

Measure the mattress, bed frame, wall, door swing, closet access, and walking paths. A bed can technically fit while still making the room difficult to use, so plan the full footprint.

For storage pieces, check both width and drawer-extension clearance. Tall chests save floor space; wider dressers provide more surface area and can support a mirror.

BEDROOM FURNITURE FAQ

WHAT PIECES ARE USUALLY INCLUDED IN A BEDROOM SET?

Bedroom-set contents vary. A listing may include a bed, dresser, mirror, chest, or nightstand in different combinations, so rely on the product description rather than the room photo.

HOW DO I KNOW WHAT BED SIZE TO ORDER?

Match the bed to your mattress size and confirm the frame's outside dimensions. Upholstered and panel beds can extend several inches beyond the mattress on each side.

SHOULD I CHOOSE A DRESSER OR A CHEST?

A dresser is wider and provides more top surface; a chest is taller and uses less floor space. Choose based on wall width, storage needs, and drawer clearance.
